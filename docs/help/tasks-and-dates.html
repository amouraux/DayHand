Help · Stacks, reminders and deadlines

Where a task lives, and when to look at it

A stack says what you intend to do with a task. A date says when you want to be reminded of it. DayHand keeps the two apart on purpose.

The five stacks

Inbox
What you have just captured and not yet placed.
Now
What you are actively working on, even if it takes several days.
Next
What you will pick up when you have room. It does not mean tomorrow.
Later
Ideas and plans you want to keep, and are not touching yet.
Waiting
Work that needs a reply, a delivery, a decision or someone else’s part before you can go on. Different from Later: here you would carry on if you could.

A date never moves a task. Time passing changes nothing about where it sits; you decide when it moves.

Here is one task over a week. On Monday you add “Prepare the presentation”, with a reminder for Tuesday and a deadline of Friday. Tuesday the reminder tells you to start, so you move it to Now. Something more urgent turns up, so it goes to Later with a new reminder for Wednesday. Wednesday it returns to Now. Thursday you send a draft to a colleague and park it in Waiting. Friday the feedback arrives, you finish it, and you tick it off. One task, one deadline that never changed, and nothing was ever “overdue”.

Reminders: look at this again

Pick Remind me… from a task’s actions, or set one in the editor. Change reminder… picks another day, and Clear the reminder removes it.

A reminder goes off at 9 a.m. on the day you chose, provided notifications are allowed for DayHand. If you set one for today after 9, there is nothing left to fire: the task is marked as due now and no notification follows. The date picker says so when that happens. (Reminders made by older versions may keep the time they were given.)

Tap a notification and DayHand opens that task. Swiping it away is not an answer; the reminder is still waiting for you in the app. You answer it by Keep here (it is in the right stack, thanks), by moving the task, by completing it, or by choosing another day.

Colours, the flag and the badge

  • Orange edge: the reminder is coming within the next three days.
  • Red edge: the reminder is today, or already past. It means “you wanted to look at this”, not “you are late”.
  • The flag in the app: gathers every task with a red or orange edge, from any stack. The number on it counts the red ones.
  • The badge on the app icon: counts reminders that have arrived and that you have not answered yet.

So the flag and the badge can disagree, and that is fine. Answer a reminder with Keep here and the badge goes down, while the task keeps its red edge and stays in the flag until you change or clear the date.

Deadlines: finished by then

Add a deadline in the editor when there is a real finish line: a submission, a renewal, a delivery. To add a new one you set a reminder first, and the deadline field appears after it. If you later clear the reminder, a deadline you already set stays visible and editable.

A deadline sends no notification and moves nothing. It is simply written on the task. Set the reminder early enough to do something about it.
