DayHand Help · Stacks and dates

Give every task a place

A stack expresses your intention. A date brings something to your attention or records a real finish date.

The five stacks

Inbox
New tasks you have captured and still need to place.
Now
Work you are actively doing, even when it takes several days.
Next
What you intend to pick up next. It does not mean tomorrow.
Later
Work and ideas you want to keep for the future.
Waiting
Work that needs a reply, delivery, decision or another contribution before you can continue. It is different from choosing to leave something for Later.

Dates never move tasks between stacks. You decide when work changes state.

Reminders: bring this back to my attention

Choose Remind me… from a task’s actions, or set a reminder in the editor. Use Change reminder… to choose another day and Clear the reminder when it is no longer needed.

New reminders notify at 9 a.m. on the chosen day, if notifications are allowed. Setting one for today after 9 a.m. marks it as due now but does not produce a notification later that day; the date picker explains this. Reminders created by older versions may retain their original time.

Opening a notification takes you to that task. Swiping the notification away does not answer the reminder. Choose Keep here to acknowledge it without moving the task, move it to another stack, complete it, or change its reminder date.

Colours, the flag and the app badge

  • Orange edge: the reminder is within the next three days.
  • Red edge: the reminder date is today or in the past. It describes the reminder date, not a missed completion deadline.
  • The flag in the app: shows tasks with red or orange edges across the open stacks. Its number counts red ones.
  • The app icon badge: counts reminders that have arrived and have not been answered.

Answering a reminder can reduce the icon badge while leaving the task’s red edge and flag count intact. The reminder date stays until you change or clear it. These counts therefore need not match.

Deadlines: this must be finished by then

Add a deadline in the task editor when there is a genuine finish date. For a new deadline, set a reminder first to reveal the deadline option. A deadline remains visible if you later clear the reminder.

A deadline does not send a separate notification or move a task. Use a reminder early enough to give yourself time to act.

Missing a notification? See notification troubleshooting.
