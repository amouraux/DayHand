DayHand Help · Getting started

Your first tasks

Start with a few real tasks. You can add projects and categories when they become useful.

1. Capture something

Choose +, enter a task, and save it. Inbox is the starting place unless you select another stack. A task can stand on its own: a project and dates are optional.

2. Give it a place

Tap or click a task to open its actions and choose where it belongs. Move something you are actively working on to Now. It can stay there for days or weeks. Use Next for what you intend to pick up next, Later for future work, and Waiting when progress depends on someone or something else.

Learn more about stacks and dates.

3. Keep useful details together

Open a task’s actions and choose Edit to change its details and Notes. Use a project to gather related tasks, a category for an area such as Home or Work, and a group to gather related projects.

A project belongs to one category and can belong to a group. Changing the project’s organisation affects the project as a whole. Tasks without a project can still have a category.

4. Focus on the work in front of you

Use search and filters to find tasks or focus on a project. On Mac and in a wide iPad layout, the sidebar gives you access to categories, groups and projects. Organise provides the controls for managing them.

In More → Appearance, choose Automatic, Light or Dark. Automatic follows your device.

5. Complete and review

Use the circular checkbox when the work is finished. Completed tasks remain available in the completion history; they can be reopened. More → Timeline shows completed work by date.

When your lists need attention, open one of the review helpers. Finishing a review means you have considered your tasks, not that you have completed them.

Try this: add “Plan the garden”, move it to Now while you explore ideas, then Waiting when you request a contractor’s quote. Set a reminder for the day you want to follow up.
