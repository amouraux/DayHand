DayHand Help · Free and paid

Access to DayHand

DayHand’s planned model is a free download with a single one-time unlock. There is no planned subscription.

Current beta: the purchase, 30-day trial and Restore Purchases flows are not yet available. Normal access is currently unlocked. The allowances below describe the planned release; a beta testing control may let you preview them.

The planned free version

Unlimited tasks, categories and groups, up to three active projects, and the last 30 days of completed history. Task and project reviews, notes, reminders, deadlines, sync, imports, exports and backups are included.

The planned one-time unlock

Unlimited active projects and full completed history across iPhone, iPad and Mac using the same Apple Account. Price and purchase availability will be shown when the purchase is ready.

What happens at a limit?

Archived projects do not count. Archiving a finished project frees a place; unarchiving uses a place. Existing projects remain readable and editable, including when an import brings you above the limit. Capturing tasks stays available.

Older completed tasks are retained even when the history view is limited. Search, backups and CSV exports keep access to the data. Nothing is deleted if you choose not to upgrade.

Trial and restoring a purchase

A user-started 30-day trial is planned, ending by returning to the free version without an automatic charge. It is not available yet. Instructions for starting it and restoring a purchase will be added when those features ship.

Help checked against the app on 31 October 2026. Labels may differ in older builds or other languages.
