What is different about it

Nothing rolls over

An undated card stays where you put it. A card in Tomorrow is still in Tomorrow next week. Time passing never moves it — only you do.

A date files a card, once it arrives

Give a card a date and it sits where it is until that day comes round, then files itself into Today or Tomorrow — and says so, since that is the one move you did not make yourself.

Tags for the work you keep returning to

Type #TRIP and the card carries that project: a coloured prefix, a category filled in for you, and a name you can change on every card at once.

Your cards stay yours

Two devices sync through one file you pick in your own iCloud Drive. No account, no server, nothing to sign up for. English, French, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese.
