What makes it different

You decide what happens today

A card stays in Inbox, Today, Tomorrow or Later until you move it. Nothing is filed on your behalf and nothing reshuffles overnight, so the list you left is the list you come back to.

Made for work that takes more than a day

Most real jobs are not one sitting. A card can stay in Today for as long as the work takes, with nothing to reschedule each morning. Today is the pile you are working from, not a promise about when you will be finished.

Remember when to start. Keep sight of when to finish.

A reminder says when to pick a task up. Once one is set you can add an optional deadline, recording the day the work has to be done. The card's coloured edge follows the reminder, not the deadline: orange within three days, red on the day itself and after. What you see is when to begin.

A reminder leaves the decision with you

When one arrives you can move the card, snooze it until tomorrow, or clear the reminder. Swiping the notification away is not a decision — the card is still there in the app afterwards, waiting for one.

Keep related work together

Type #TRIP and the card carries that project: a coloured prefix, a category filled in for you, and a name you can change on every card at once. Categories, groups and filters hold the rest together without crowding the main list.

Your cards stay yours

Everything is kept on your device. Two of them sync through a single file you pick in your own iCloud Drive — no DayHand account, no server, nothing to sign up for. Import and export as CSV, and a backup is taken automatically each day.

See what you have accomplished

Any to-do app can show what you owe. DayHand also shows what you got done — this week and last, grouped by the day you finished it. The counterweight to a list that otherwise only ever looks forward: progress measured as what you finished.

Getting it

iPhone and iPad

iOS 17 or later

  1. Install Apple's TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the link below on the device. It hands over to TestFlight.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install.
Join the beta

Mac

macOS 14 or later

  1. Install TestFlight from the Mac App Store — it is a separate app from the iPhone one, and the same Apple Account has to be signed in.
  2. Open the same link below on the Mac. Safari hands it to TestFlight.
  3. Click Accept, then Install. It lands in your Applications folder like any other app.

One link for both — it is one app and one target, not a separate Mac version. If the link opens the App Store instead of TestFlight, TestFlight is not installed on that Mac yet.

Get it on Mac

Already running DayHand on your Mac from a build you made yourself? The TestFlight build is sandboxed — App Store Connect will not take one that is not — and a sandboxed app keeps its data somewhere else. It will open with no cards, which looks alarming and is not: your old file is untouched where it always was. If you sync through iCloud Drive, open More → Use Existing Sync File… and pick the same file again, and everything comes back.
