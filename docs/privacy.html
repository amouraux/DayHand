Privacy Policy

Your tasks belong in your own files. DayHand does not send your task content to the developer or operate a server that stores it.

Overview

This policy covers DayHand for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and explains the separate handling of information you choose to share through support or this website.

The app has no DayHand account, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. The developer does not receive your task lists, notes or imported reminders through normal use of the app. DayHand does not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Tasks, settings and backups

Tasks, notes, projects, categories, groups, dates and completion information are stored in a DayHand document on your device. The app also stores settings and review progress locally. This information is used to provide task management, search, reviews and recovery.

DayHand creates local backups and lets you make and share additional copies. Backups and exports can contain the same private information as your task list. Their filenames can include a device name. They remain in the locations you or the app saved them to; they are not uploaded to the developer.

DayHand documents and CSV exports are readable files; the app does not add its own password protection or encryption to them. Access depends on your device security, file permissions and the storage service you choose.

Sync through your iCloud Drive

If you enable sync, DayHand reads and writes the file you select in your iCloud Drive. Apple transfers that file between your devices under your iCloud account. The file does not pass through a DayHand server, and enabling sync does not grant the developer access to it.

Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your account settings. If you put a file in a shared folder or share a copy with someone, that person may be able to read its contents.

Importing tasks

The Apple Reminders importer asks for access to read your lists. DayHand uses that permission to copy the reminders you select and does not edit the originals. Imported content, including supported notes and other details, becomes part of your DayHand files.

Todoist and DayHand CSV imports read files you select on your device. DayHand does not ask for your Todoist password or connect to your Todoist account. These imports are one-time copies, not ongoing connections to the original service.

Reminders and notifications

DayHand schedules local notifications through Apple’s operating system. Notification content can include a task’s title, which may appear on your lock screen depending on your notification settings. DayHand does not send task titles to a developer-operated notification server.

Apple services and purchases

Apple handles App Store downloads, TestFlight distribution and any diagnostic information you choose to share through Apple’s settings. Apple may make diagnostic reports or beta feedback available to the developer. DayHand does not include an additional analytics or crash-reporting service.

The current beta does not yet provide the planned purchase, trial or purchase-restoration flows. When available, purchases will be processed by Apple, and DayHand will use StoreKit purchase information to determine access on the device. The developer will not receive your payment-card details through the app. This policy will be updated if the way purchase data is handled changes.

Your privacy choices and deletion

  • Use DayHand locally: sync is not required. To disconnect a linked file, choose More → Sync → Stop Using This File. This keeps your local tasks and does not delete the shared file.
  • Revoke Reminders access: change DayHand’s Reminders permission in your device’s system settings. Revoking permission does not remove tasks already imported; delete those separately if you no longer want them.
  • Control notifications: disable notifications or hide their previews in system settings. You can also clear individual task reminders in DayHand.
  • Export your work: use More → Tasks → Export as CSV, or share a backup from More → Backups.
  • Delete tasks: delete them in the app. When sync is enabled, deletions can propagate to connected devices. Older backups and exported copies can still contain the deleted content.
  • Remove saved copies: delete backups from More → Backups and delete any exports or shared files you no longer need in Files or Finder. Check other devices and any storage service’s recently deleted items or recovery features too.

Active task data is retained until you change or delete it. Automatic backups rotate; manual backups and copies you share remain until replaced or deleted. Deletion records used for sync may retain task identifiers and deletion dates, rather than task content. Removing the app does not remove separately saved iCloud files, exports or copies on other devices.

There is no DayHand account to close. The developer cannot retrieve or remotely erase task files that remain solely on your devices or in your storage account.

Information you choose to send and how to contact us

For questions about this policy or a privacy request, contact the DayHand developer through the support page. During the beta you can send feedback through TestFlight. GitHub Issues is also available for general questions and bug reports.

If you send feedback, a diagnostic report or a support message, the developer receives the information you include and any information supplied by that channel, such as a contact address, device details or attachments. This information is used to respond, troubleshoot and improve DayHand. Support records may be retained as needed to resolve the request and follow up on the issue; contact the developer through the same channel to request deletion of information you supplied. Apple and GitHub manage their own copies and retention under their policies.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post private tasks, backups, credentials or personal details there. Use an invented example when possible. For a privacy request, start without sensitive details and ask for an appropriate way to follow up.

This website and external services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add analytics scripts or advertising cookies. GitHub may process connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate and secure hosting. GitHub Issues and external links are covered by the respective providers’ policies: GitHub privacy statement and Apple privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Material changes to the app’s data handling will be reflected here and, where required, in the app’s notices and App Store privacy disclosures.
